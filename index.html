Alkadiet — legal

Alkadiet estimates the acid or base load of your food using PRAL, a peer-reviewed nutritional metric. It is a food tracker, not a medical device, and it never shows a pH value.

  • Privacy Policy — what stays on your device, the three things that leave it, and why
  • Terms of Use — licence, subscription, and the limits of what the app can tell you

The short version of the privacy policy: your food log lives on your device and in your own private iCloud. We run no user database and cannot read your log. Nothing is sold, no ads, no cross-app tracking.
